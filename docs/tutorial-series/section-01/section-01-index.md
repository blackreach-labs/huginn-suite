## Section Summary

Section 1 introduces the Huginn enterprise penetration testing framework and prepares operators to work with the platform. These four videos cover platform orientation, installation, interface navigation, and licensing — everything needed to begin productive engagement work with Huginn.

All videos in this section are available in the **Free** tier. No Professional or Enterprise license is required to follow along with any content in Section 1.

### Video Breakdown

- **Video 1** explains what Huginn is — a professional-grade enterprise pentesting framework — its target audience (working penetration testers, red team operators, security consultancies, and newcomers entering the field), the six-phase attack chain methodology, and key differentiators from fragmented multi-tool workflows
- **Video 2** covers system requirements (Python 3.10+, Windows, 8GB RAM), installation via pip and from source, first launch, workspace configuration, and common troubleshooting
- **Video 3** provides an efficiency-focused UI orientation — the sidebar, attack chain toolbar, session management for multi-engagement tracking, module navigation across 270+ tools, and the guided workflow versus manual mode distinction
- **Video 4** breaks down the three license tiers (Free, Professional at $99/month, Enterprise at $299/month), demonstrates license activation, and recommends which tier fits which operational profile

### Prerequisites

None — this is the starting point for the entire series.

### Who This Section Is For

- **Professional pentesters** setting up Huginn for engagement work
- **Red team operators** evaluating the platform for adversary simulation operations
- **Security consultancies** onboarding their team onto the framework
- **Newcomers to penetration testing** who want to learn on a professional-grade tool from day one
