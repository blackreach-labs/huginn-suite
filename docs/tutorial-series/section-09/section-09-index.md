## Section Summary

Section 9 covers reporting and documentation capabilities using Huginn's Report phase modules. These 5 videos demonstrate how to manage findings, generate reports in multiple formats, produce executive summaries, map results to compliance frameworks, and analyze trends across scan history.

This section features a mix of **Free** and **Enterprise** tier tools. The core reporting workflow — managing findings and generating standard reports — is available to all users. Enterprise features add executive-level reporting, compliance framework mapping, and predictive trend analysis for organizations with advanced documentation requirements.

Reporting is the final operational phase in the attack chain methodology: after reconnaissance, scanning, exploitation, and privilege escalation, testers must document their findings in a clear, actionable format for stakeholders. This section bridges technical results with business-facing deliverables.

### Prerequisites

- Video 26: Results Interpretation — understanding scan results and evidence review before documenting findings
- Completed scans with findings from earlier sections (Sections 4–6, 8) to have data for report generation
